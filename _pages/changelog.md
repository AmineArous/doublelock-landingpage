# Changelog

### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**
**October 2026**

Welcome to **Double Lock**! Escape the traps of Victor Lenoir, the greatest art forger of his time.

#### Features

- 🗝️ **Four Scenes** — Victor's study in Paris, a night train to Venice, a secret studio on a canal and a yacht on the Adriatic
- 🎬 **A Film-Noir Story** — An art forger, an investigative journalist and a chase across Europe
- 🧩 **Puzzles in Every Object** — Safes, chess, mirror writing, a magic lantern, a lighthouse in the night
- 🎨 **A New Artist Each Time** — In Venice, every game draws one of eight painters
- 💡 **One Hint at a Time** — Matched to where you are, never the answer
- 🔓 **First Scene Free** — Unlock the full game with a single purchase
